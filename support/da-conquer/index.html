Da Conquer Support

Support for Da Conquer

Da Conquer can be played online in a web browser and is available on Google Play for Android. The iPhone and iPad release is currently in Apple review. Da SayKa is the beginner/training mode inside Da Conquer.

Contact support

For Da Conquer or Da SayKa questions, testing feedback, or issue reports, email Da Mystro Gamings LLC.

damystrogamings21@gmail.com

What to include

Include “Da Conquer” or “Da SayKa” in your message, your device type, app/build version if known, and a clear description of the question or issue. Do not send passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal information by email.

Android testing download

Download Da Conquer 1.1.5 (15) directly to your Android phone. No tester invitation or separate testing app is needed. Android 7.1 or newer; download size 127 MB.

Download Android APK · 1.1.5 (15)

This Android build includes updated login behavior, Joker finishes, Da Meskel scoring displays, and winning animations. The browser edition is a separate version.

Installing on your phone

Open the downloaded APK on your Android phone and follow the installation prompts. Android may ask you to allow installation from the browser or Files app you used.

If installation reports a conflict with your Google Play copy, do not uninstall your existing game: local progress could be lost. Use the Google Play update or contact support instead.
