Injera Be Wote Privacy

Privacy information for Injera Be Wote

Injera Be Wote is available as a player-versus-AI web game and as an Android app. Last updated: July 31, 2026.

Current status

The current release provides player-versus-AI gameplay. Online multiplayer is marked as coming soon and cannot be opened. The game does not require an account, collect a child's name, or offer purchases, chat, cloud saves, or social features.

Information stored on the device

A player chooses a local display name for gameplay against the computer. That display name, game settings, level progress, and saved matches remain on the player's device and are not transmitted to Da Mystro Gamings. A player or parent can remove this information by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Advertising in the Android app

The public Android version may show one non-personalized, child-directed advertisement before a match. Ads are provided by the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google may automatically receive an approximate location derived from the device's IP address, app interactions such as app launches, taps, or ad views, diagnostic information, and app or device identifiers such as the app set ID. Google uses this information to provide advertising, measure performance, and prevent fraud. This information is encrypted in transit. The Android advertising ID is disabled for this child-directed app.

Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services in Google's partner technologies notice.

Web and school versions

The website version and the separate school build do not show ads. They store the same gameplay settings and progress locally on the device and do not transmit that gameplay information to Da Mystro Gamings.

Future changes

This page will be updated before a future release adds online accounts, multiplayer, cloud storage, or other data practices.
