Injera Be Wote Privacy

Privacy information for Injera Be Wote

Injera Be Wote is currently available as a player-versus-AI web game. Last updated: July 31, 2026.

Current status

The current release provides offline-style player-versus-AI gameplay in a web browser. Online multiplayer is marked as coming soon and cannot be opened.

Current data practices

The current game does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, marketing tracking, cloud saves, or gameplay tracking. Player settings, level progress, and saved matches are stored locally on the player's device. The game does not transmit that local gameplay information to Da Mystro Gamings.

Future changes

This page will be updated before any future release adds online accounts, multiplayer, analytics, advertising, cloud storage, or other data collection.

Support contact

For privacy questions, access requests, deletion requests, education inquiries, or Injera Be Wote support, contact damystrogamings21@gmail.com.
