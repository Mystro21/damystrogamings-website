Privacy information for Da Conquer

Da Conquer is available now on Google Play for Android. The iPhone and iPad release is currently in Apple review. Last updated: September 4, 2026.

Current status

Da Conquer is publicly available for Android through Google Play. Its iPhone and iPad release is currently in Apple review. Da SayKa is the beginner/training mode inside Da Conquer, not a separate app.

Current data practices

Da Conquer may collect app activity, app information and performance data, and device or other identifiers to operate, support, improve, and monetize the game. The app does not sell personal information. Data is encrypted in transit, and the current Google Play disclosure states that data is not shared with third parties.
